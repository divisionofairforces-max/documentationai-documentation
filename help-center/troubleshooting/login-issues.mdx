description: Quick solutions for login problems.
---

## Issue
- Can't access FTO portal
- Error: "Invalid username or password"

## Quick Fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Click "Forgot password" at `https://fto.dpd.com/portal` and follow the prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check invite email">
    Make sure you activated your account from the FTO email invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If you still can't log in, email `fto-admin@dpd.com` for help.
  </Step>
</Steps>
