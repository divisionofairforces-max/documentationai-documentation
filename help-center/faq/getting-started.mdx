## Portal Access

DPD FTO Program participants receive an invite email.

**Steps:**

1. Check your inbox for an email from `fto-admin@dpd.com`.
2. Click the activation link inside to set your password.
3. Visit `https://fto.dpd.com/portal` and log in with your credentials.

**Tip:**
- Bookmark the link for easy access.
- Contact support if you did not receive an invite.
