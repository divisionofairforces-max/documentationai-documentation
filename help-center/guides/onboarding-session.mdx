## Book Your Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the FTO portal">
    Go to `https://fto.dpd.com/portal` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Onboarding">
    Click "Onboarding" in the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date and time">
    Select a session slot that fits your schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm booking">
    Review your details and click "Book".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You’ll get a calendar invite with details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
