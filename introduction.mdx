## How to organize your content

A simple structure works best for growing documentation. Start with an overview page, then add focused sections for setup, processes, troubleshooting, and reference material. This keeps the experience predictable and makes it easier for readers to move from general context to specific actions.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep each page narrowly focused. If a topic grows too large, split it into smaller pages so readers can scan and update content more easily.
</Callout>

## Navigation tips

Name pages in plain language and keep the menu structure shallow. Readers should be able to guess where a topic lives without searching through multiple folders. When possible, group related content together and reuse the same terms across headings, links, and labels.
